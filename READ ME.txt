------Electricity Bill Management System---------
this project focuses on managing electricity bill. Works for both employees and customers with different interfaces for each.

Features : customer management, billing, paid status, late fee, employee and customer logins.

How to compile and run:
gcc main.c customer.c billing.c input.c -o billsystem
.\billsystem
